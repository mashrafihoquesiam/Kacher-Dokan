# V19 Seller Category & Product Options

Seller Add Product improvements on web and mobile:

- Expanded category choices:
  - Rice & Grains
  - Lentils & Pulses
  - Dairy & Eggs
  - Oil & Ghee
  - Spices & Condiments
  - Fruits & Vegetables
  - Meat & Fish
  - Bakery & Bread
  - Snacks & Biscuits
  - Beverages
  - Tea & Coffee
  - Frozen Foods
  - Instant Foods
  - Personal Care
  - Baby Care
  - Household & Cleaning
  - Stationery
  - Pet Care
  - Electronics & Accessories
  - Others / Custom
- Selecting Others / Custom reveals a field where the seller can enter any category.
- Category choices are displayed in English or Bangla based on the selected UI language.
- Added optional Brand field.
- Added optional Product Details / description field.
- Brand and product details are saved to MongoDB and visible on customer product cards.
- Existing product editor can update brand and product details as well.
- Existing image, stock, price, unit, availability, restock and delete controls remain unchanged.
